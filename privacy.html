Privacy Policy

Paper Empires uploader. Last updated: November 3, 2026

What this application is

The Paper Empires uploader is a private application run by the owner of the Paper Empires YouTube channel on their own computer. It is not offered to the public: the only Google account that authorizes it is the channel owner's.

Google data it accesses

Through the YouTube API Services, and only with the permissions the channel owner grants, the application:

  • uploads videos, thumbnails and captions to the owner's channel, and manages its playlists, titles, descriptions and comments;
  • reads the channel's own statistics (views, watch time, audience retention, subscribers, thumbnail impressions and click-through rate);
  • reads the channel's estimated revenue, once the channel is in the YouTube Partner Program.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to publish the channel's videos and to decide which topics and titles work best for future videos. It is stored only on the owner's computer, together with the authorization token. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Third-party terms

This application uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer also removes the application's access.
